What is Flute Notation?

Flute Notation is a beginner-friendly website that helps flute players find sargam notation for their favourite songs. Instead of reading Western sheet music, you follow syllable-based notes — Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni — presented directly below each line of lyrics.

🎶

Who is it for?

This site is designed for beginner and intermediate flute players who play a 6-hole bamboo or PVC flute (bansuri-style flute). Whether you are starting out or adding new songs to your repertoire, Flute Notation makes learning intuitive and comfortable.

🔍

How to Use

Go to the Home page and type a song name in the search bar. Click Search to find matching songs. Click any song card to open the clean music notation sheet with lyrics and sargam notes. You can also browse all songs on the Songs page.

🎼 The Notation System

🎵
System
Indian Sargam (Sa Re Ga Ma …)
🕳️
Flute Type
Designed for 6-Hole Flute (bansuri-style). Each song shows its specific flute type — always check before playing.
📝
Format
Lyric appears on line 1, and its corresponding sargam notation appears directly on line 2, line-by-line in a clean music-sheet format.
⚠️
Important
Notation accuracy depends on your flute's tuning and key. Always verify with your own instrument.

Sargam Notes

Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Da Ni Sa' --

Sa' = upper octave Sa  |  -- = hold / extend the note
